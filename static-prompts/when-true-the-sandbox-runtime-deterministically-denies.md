When true, the sandbox runtime deterministically denies hosts not in allowedDomains instead of prompting. Enforced for sandboxed commands only — in-process tools such as WebFetch are not gated by this setting. Only honored from user, managed/policy, or CLI (--settings) settings — ${…} are ignored, and while it is on their allowedDomains and WebFetch(domain:…) allow rules are left out of the allowlist.
