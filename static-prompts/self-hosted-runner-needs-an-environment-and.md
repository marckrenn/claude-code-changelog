A self-hosted runner needs an environment and its key.
If you already have an environment key, paste it here and press Enter. Otherwise:

1. In a browser, open ${…}/admin-settings/cloud-environments and
   make sure the Allow self-hosted environments setting is on.
2. In the Self-hosted environments section, click New, enter a name, and click Create.
3. Click Copy environment key, paste the key here, and press Enter.

Nothing shows as you paste, and the key isn’t sent to Claude.

If the key is in a file, you’ve lost it, or you’d like help from Claude,
type help and press Enter.
